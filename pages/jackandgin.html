How to Play

Wager your Tribute and click Deal. You and Moloch each get three cards. Build melds. Beat his score.

Your Turn

Draw another card (Hit) or lock in your hand (Stand). On your first three cards only, Double matches your wager, takes exactly one more card, and ends your turn.

Scoring — Melds, Rummy-Style

Only cards inside a meld score. Unused cards are deadwood and pay nothing — but they don't cost you either.

  • Set — three or four of the same rank.
  • Run — three or more consecutive ranks. Ace plays low or high.
  • Flush — three or more cards in the same suit.
  • Flush Run — a run that is also a flush. Multipliers stack.

Four-card melds pay ×1.5. Five or more pay ×2. Same-suit runs pay an additional ×1.5.

Bust

If your hand total reaches 40 or more, you bust and Moloch wins — no matter what melds you had.

Moloch's Turn

When you stand, Moloch flips his cards and keeps drawing until he strictly beats you. He has no fear of busting — losing-by-bust is no worse to him than losing-by-score.

Payout

Win — wager pays 2×. Push — tied score, both bust, or no melds on either side: wager returned. Loss — wager forfeit.

The Jackpot

Every time you lose, 1% of your Tribute (at least one coin) is dropped into the jackpot. It accrues across sessions. To claim it, win a round with a meld score of 40 or more — the pot pays out on top of your normal win.
