How it works

Set a wager. Crawl the baby across the road with arrow keys or WASD. Reach the curb to clear a level.

  • Multiplier grows ×0.5 per level: ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5, … up to ×5 at level 10. Cashing at L1 is a loss — you have to push.
  • After a clear: cash out your pot, or push it to a longer, faster road.
  • Get hit by a car → baby is squished. Wager and pot, gone. No retries.
