ANDERSON COUNTY · TENNESSEE · WEDNESDAY 2004 · FIFTY CENTS

Local Woman Dead After Domestic Incident

Husband in Custody · TBI Investigating · Child Safe With Family

By Staff

Anderson County Sheriff's deputies responded to a residence on the south end of Oak Ridge late Tuesday evening and took the homeowner into custody following the death of his wife. The case has been referred to the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation. Names are being withheld pending notification of family.

A minor child was reported to be in the care of relatives at the time of the incident and was not present at the residence. Sheriff's deputies and DCS personnel responded jointly. The District Attorney's office has been advised. An autopsy is scheduled.
