He bought it himself at a flea market in Sweetwater. He was never a Marine. He liked Marines movies, Marvel, anything with heroes. You never knew if thought him self one or wished for one.

He kept it on the kitchen counter and you knew where it was without thinking, like you know where the salt is.

When you needed, it was where it always was.

You flicked the wheel. Louder than drunken snoring. It caught on the first try, the way his never did.

You did not stay to see what happened next but you could smell it as you left, like breakfast.
