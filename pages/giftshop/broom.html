EXT. FROZEN FOREST — DAY

SANKO stops. Her face stretches. Stretches. Eyes slide too far apart. The mouth gapes. She is NEFARIOUS LADY MATCHA now.

EXT. ROCKS BELOW — CONTINUOUS
