How it works

Pick a side — bear or dogs — and stake your purse. The bookie posts odds, but he isn't always right.

  • The fight resolves in real time. Bear dies → dogs win. All dogs die → bear wins.
  • You see what the bookie sees, and a little more. Read the pit. Read the form sheet. Bears have tells. Dogs have tells. The bookie reads the obvious ones.
  • If you back the bear and lose, your stake is gone. Same for the dogs.
