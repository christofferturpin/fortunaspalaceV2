How the Derby Works

Each animal carries two Wikipedia articles on its back to Noah's Ark. The pair with the most popular pages over the last 30 days reaches Noah first. The rest are washed away by God's righteous judgement!

Make Your Picks

  • Click three animals to fill the Win, Place, and Show slots.
  • Choose a tithe per pick. Your total offering is tithe × 3.
  • Pageview totals are hidden until the race ends. Pick on faith, not reason.

How Noah Pays

WIN 1st → 6× · 2nd → 3× · 3rd → 1.85× · 4th-9th washed away
PLACE 1st or 2nd → 3× · 3rd → 1.85× · 4th-9th washed away
SHOW 1st, 2nd, or 3rd → 1.85× · else washed away

A Win pick that only places still pays — at the Place rate. A Win or Place pick that only shows still pays — at the Show rate. May God bless all his creatures.
