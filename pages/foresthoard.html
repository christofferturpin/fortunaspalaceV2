About Shareware Poker…

The Deck

38 cards: ranks 1–6 in three suits, two copies of every (rank, suit) pair, plus two wild cards. Wilds substitute for any rank in any suit; the classifier picks the highest-paying legal interpretation.

How to Play

  1. Place a bet, click Deal. Ten cards are dealt: five into the Left hand, five into the Right. All ten start marked DISCARD by default.
  2. Click any card to mark it HOLD (click again to un-mark). Click Draw — un-held cards in both hands are replaced. You get exactly one draw.
  3. After the draw you have one swap: click a card to arm it, then click any second card to exchange their slots — including across the LEFT/RIGHT line. (Drag-and-drop also works.) Same-side reorders are free.
  4. Click Score when ready. Both hands are scored against the paytable.

Suggest Button

The Suggest button applies the best play for you. In the hold phase it picks which cards to keep through the draw; in the swap phase it performs the optimal cross-side swap. It costs 50% of the potential winnings it unlocks (minimum 1 coin), charged from your bank.

Winning

You must hit a paying hand on BOTH sides. The floor is Two Pair; anything weaker pays nothing on that side, and a single empty side voids the whole round.

When both sides qualify, the payout formula is (left × + right × − 2) × bet. The −2 is the house's share. That means any pair of floor hands (Two Pair / Straight / Three of a Kind) on both sides busts — the house tax eats them. You need one side at Flush or better to scrape breakeven, and profit kicks in beyond that. Matching Royal Flushes on both sides return 18× the bet.

Hand Rankings

Standard 5-card poker. Royal Flush is the top straight flush (2-6 same suit). Five of a Kind is only reachable through a wild. Pair and high-card hands don't pay.
