Stroke of Luck

Three dice. Three picks. A win earns you putts.

Coins 0
How it works

Three dice are rolled. Each die has two red faces and four black faces:

  • Die 1 — red on 1 2, black on 3-6
  • Die 2 — red on 3 4, black on 1, 2, 5, 6
  • Die 3 — red on 5 6, black on 1-4

Pick one from each of the three rows, then roll. A top-row hit opens a putt-putt course where every ball you sink pays out; a top-row miss loses the wager outright.

  • Top row — number of balls you get on the course (miss → no game).
  • Middle row — per-ball value multiplier on a hit (×1.5 / ×2 / ×3). On a middle miss every sunk ball just pays back the wager.
  • Bottom row — flat % of wager bonus on a hit. Pct = (100 − hit%) / 2, so the rarer pick pays more (No Doubles +22%, Doubles +28%).

Per sunk ball = wager × (middle hit ? middle.mult : 1). Missed balls pay nothing. Cost of a roll is just the wager. Bottom-row bonus is added on top of putt winnings.

If you miss the same course 9 times in a row, the next ball gets a fresh layout — same ball budget, new hallway.

The Jackpot

Every losing roll drops 1% of your Tribute (at least one coin) into the jackpot. It accrues across sessions and survives death. To claim it, roll 1-1-1 or 6-6-6 — the pot pays out on top of whatever the roll resolves to, even if your top bet missed.
