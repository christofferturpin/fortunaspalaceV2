🌿 ForestTrick! 🌿

A little game for three friends in the small wood — pay no mind to what the moss remembers.
Rules

Deck

Each of the 3 players (You, Winter, Summer) holds a personal 38-card deck: ranks 1–6 in three suits — 🐟 fish, 🦊 fox, 🦉 owl — with two copies of every (rank, suit) pair, plus two wild cards: 🐝 bee and 🧒 child. A wild can stand in for any rank in any suit; the classifier picks the best legal interpretation of your selection.

Flow

You always lead the first trick. After that, the winner of each trick leads the next. Within a trick, each player in turn order commits a play (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 cards forming a legal hand). Followers see prior plays before deciding.

Must-beat rule: if a follower can construct any hand that beats the current top of the table, they must play a beating hand. If they can't beat it, they're free to play anything legal — donate a junk single, conserve material, whatever makes sense.

Hand ladder — weakest to strongest

  1. Single — one card
  2. Pair — two of the same rank
  3. Two pair — two pairs of different ranks (4 cards)
  4. Three of a kind — three of the same rank
  5. Straight — five consecutive ranks (only 1–5 or 2–6)
  6. Flush — five of the same suit, non-consecutive
  7. Full house — three of one rank + two of another
  8. Four of a kind — four of the same rank (4-card play; top of the 4-card tier above two pair)
  9. Straight flush — five consecutive of the same suit

Resolving the trick

The strongest hand wins. Within the same type, higher rank wins. If two or more hands tie on type and rank, look inside the hand: highest card in the set wins, then the next card, then the next — for full house this means the kicker (the pair part) breaks ties after the triple. Suits rank fish < fox < owl. If two hands are still completely card-for-card identical (only possible when wilds substitute the same way), the seat that played first in turn order takes the trick.

Collecting

The winner sweeps every card played this trick — their own play plus everyone else's — into a personal collected pile that doesn't cycle back. Everyone refills their hand back up to 10 from their own draw pile.

Game end & scoring

The game ends the moment any player empties both their hand and draw pile. That player earns a +10 first-out bonus. Final score per player is:

collected cards + tricks won + (1 per wild collected) + (10 first-out)

Highest total wins. 👑

Wager

Before each game you place a coin wager from your wallet. If You win the game, the wager returns at 1.75× (your bet + 75% profit). If Winter or Summer wins, the wager is forfeit. Bee, fish, and fox don't gamble — they're hardwired to play their best.
