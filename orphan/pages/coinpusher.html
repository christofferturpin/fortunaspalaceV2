How it works

Three dice are rolled. Each die has two red faces and four black faces:

  • Die 1 — red on 1 2, black on 3-6
  • Die 2 — red on 3 4, black on 1, 2, 5, 6
  • Die 3 — red on 5 6, black on 1-4

Pick one from each of the three rows. Top sets the number of tokens minted. Bottom sets the per-token value multiplier (miss → ×1). Third makes the tokens ~33% physically bigger in the pusher if you call the dice right — bulkier coins push harder. Tokens reprice off your current wager every push, so the value travels with whatever you're betting now.

The pusher cabinet is seeded with house chips at four tiers: ×0.5, ×1, ×2, and a rare ×5. Each chip is worth that multiplier × your most recent wager, so the field reprices every time you bet. Push them off the front for full value; off the side gutters for half.
